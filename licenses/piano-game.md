# One more song

The audio is original procedural piano synthesis in `src/game/piano/audio.ts`.
No third-party sound samples, commercial recordings, or audio dependencies are
bundled. The synthesis uses inharmonic, decaying sine partials, a deterministic
hammer transient, and a generated stereo room impulse.

The following public-domain compositions have original short monophonic game
arrangements in `src/game/piano/songs.ts`. These are playable excerpts, not full
concert scores. Register and key are adjusted for the C4–D5 keyboard:

- Beethoven, *Für Elise*, WoO 59: opening theme, transposed to D minor.
  Score reference: https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BeethovenLv/WoO59/fur_Elise_WoO59/fur_Elise_WoO59-let.pdf
- Traditional, *Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star*: complete familiar melody in C.
- Beethoven, *Ode to Joy*: familiar theme in C.
- Traditional, *Mary Had a Little Lamb*: familiar melody in C, using four white
  keys (C, D, E, G) at 72 BPM.
- Traditional, *Hot Cross Buns*: three-note melody in C at 68 BPM. The repeated
  C and D notes are lengthened for a slower, beginner-friendly rhythm.
  Beginner melody references: https://gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/FirstPieces/SelectedTunes.pdf

No text or arrangements from modern commercial editions were embedded.
